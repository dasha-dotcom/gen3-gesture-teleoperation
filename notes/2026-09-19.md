# September 19, 2026 — camera-to-mock-Gen3 milestone

## Result

Mac webcam → MediaPipe palm center → UDP → Ubuntu calibrated hold-to-run bridge → MoveIt Servo → mock Gen3 is working for base-frame y/z translation. This captures progress beyond the earlier Stage 1 ROS package, which remains in the repository.

The latest isolated-z recording showed about 17.137 mm downward and 10.533 mm upward motion, 0.114 mm net y change, and about 0.029 degrees net orientation change. Final samples were stable for about three seconds. Input duration/amplitude were not matched, so unequal travel is not evidence of asymmetric gain. An earlier mixed-axis run showed about 1.4 degrees net orientation change; its cause remains unresolved.

## Design decisions captured

- Keep camera processing on macOS and ROS on Ubuntu ARM64.
- Send palm coordinates, not video; use the calibrated neutral point as the rest position.
- Use Space as hold-to-run and latch interruptions off until an explicit fresh press.
- Use symmetric y/z scaling and a combined 10 mm/s requested-speed cap.
- Preserve one current calibrated bridge and one current offset helper, without the older competing versions.
- Keep the deliberate-gap sender separate from the normal sender.

## Scope of this repository capture

The Python scripts are preserved from the exercise artifacts. Servo launch/configuration were reconstructed from the supplied exercise instructions, not fetched from the live VM. Automated checks cover syntax and isolated mapping/gating behavior. ROS/GUI observations above came from Dasha's interactive VM tests, not from running ROS on the Mac during this update.

The normal saved sender removes the deliberate pause from the exercised loop. Its fresh end-to-end run remains pending. This is a research checkpoint, not a clean-install or production-readiness claim.
